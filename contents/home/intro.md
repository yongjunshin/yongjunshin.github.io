I am a software engineering researcher continually exploring how software engineering can make Physical AI more trustworthy.

Highlights of my professional and academic experience include:

- Senior Researcher at [Electronics and Telecommunications Research Institute (ETRI)](https://www.etri.re.kr/eng/main/main.etri), a national research institute in South Korea
- Lecturer at the ETRI AI Academy
- Board Member of the Software Engineering Society, Korean Institute of Information Scientists and Engineers (KIISE)
- Member of the Daejeon Metropolitan City Informatization Committee
- Ph.D. in Software Engineering from [Korea Advanced Institute of Science and Technology (KAIST)](https://www.kaist.ac.kr/en/), under the supervision of Professor [Doo-Hwan Bae](https://cs.kaist.ac.kr/people/view?idx=12&kind=faculty&menu=170)

My research interests center on **Software Engineering for Trustworthy Physical AI**, with a particular focus on verification and validation, including:

- Simulation-Based Testing
- Formal Verification and Runtime Verification
- Abstraction and Model-Based Engineering
- Requirement Engineering and Ontology
